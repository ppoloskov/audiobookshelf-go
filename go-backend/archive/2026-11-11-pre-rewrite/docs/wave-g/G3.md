## Ranked suspects for Wave G reproduction

1. Collection deletion leaves its RSS feed row and omits `rss_feed_closed` (source-observed (pending independent reproduction), highest confidence and straightforward regression).
2. Podcast hard-delete semantics: determine which Go route owns filesystem removal and ensure a failed removal does not abort DB lifecycle, matching TS caught-error behavior.
3. Partial batch progress response/event behavior: Go appears to return 200 after silently skipping failed entries; verify TS per-entry semantics and successful-event count.
4. Podcast feed-check transient failure and next scheduled retry; verify queue de-duplication and cron recurrence without changing intended retry policy.
5. Bookmark behavior after item deletion: determine whether upstream intentionally retains user bookmark JSON; do not add cleanup unless TS proves it.
6. Failure atomicity for direct playlist remove/batch remove and collection entry reorder: current loop ignores order-update errors (`collection_http.go:439-440`); test transaction behavior and compare TS before treating as a bug.
